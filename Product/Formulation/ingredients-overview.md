> Last updated: July 2026

## Formulation Philosophy

Every functional ingredient in shroomé is there for a reason, at a dose that matters. No proprietary blends. No fairy-dusting on the actives. The full ingredient list and doses are printed on every sachet because transparency is the standard.

**Format matters:** shroomé is not a powder. Each sachet is a ready-to-pour liquid concentrate — 30 ml (1 fl oz) — that you tear and pour over the milk of your choice, hot or iced. No powder, no whisk, no mess.

## Core Ingredients

### 1. Organic Ceremonial Matcha — 2.5g per sachet

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Grade | Ceremonial |
| Supplier | Keicha (Matcha Powder 700) |
| Caffeine | about 60mg per serving (printed on pack; supplier CoA pending) |
| L-Theanine | Naturally occurring |

**Why it matters:**
- 2.5g is a meaningful ceremonial serving — many competitors use 1-2g of culinary-grade matcha and call it "matcha-powered"
- The naturally occurring L-theanine + caffeine combination promotes calm, sustained alertness rather than a jittery spike
- About 60mg caffeine is roughly half a shot of espresso (printed on pack; not yet confirmed by a supplier caffeine analysis)

**Taste contribution:** Vegetal sweetness, umami depth, the "green" backbone of the drink

**Origin note:** The short-form provenance line is **"Grown in Kyoto, Made in California."** Matcha origin is not documented on the formulation sheet (supplier "Keicha") — obtain an origin CoA before making a Kyoto-specific labeling claim.

---

### 2. Organic Lion's Mane Extract — 200mg per sachet

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Source | Lion's mane extract (Immulink) |
| Amount | 200mg of extract per sachet |
| Beta-glucan content | Not documented on the formulation sheet — do not state a % or a mg-of-beta-glucans figure until a supplier CoA is on file |

**Why it matters:**
- Lion's mane is a functional mushroom studied for its bioactive compounds
- The dose is 200mg of **extract** per sachet — the on-pack and site figure

**Compliance note:** The beta-glucan percentage is **not** substantiated by the delivered formulation sheet. Do **not** claim "≥70% beta-glucans" or "200mg beta-glucans" until Immulink provides a spec sheet / CoA. If the extract were 70% BG, actual beta-glucans would be ≈140mg — so the "200mg beta-glucans" phrasing is inaccurate regardless.

**Functional role:** Structure/function claims only (e.g. "supports immune function", "supports sustained focus").

---

### 3. Grass-Fed Collagen — 2g per sachet

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Source | Grass-fed bovine (Nutra Food Ingredients) |
| Form | Hydrolyzed collagen peptides |
| Type | Primarily Type I and Type III |

**Why it matters:**
- Hydrolyzed collagen peptides are broken down into smaller molecules for absorption
- 2g is a meaningful daily maintenance dose — while clinical studies often use 5-10g, daily consistency at 2g contributes to cumulative benefits
- Type I collagen supports skin elasticity and hydration; Type III supports skin structure

**Functional role:** Skin health support, daily collagen maintenance

## What's NOT in shroomé

| Excluded | Reason |
|---|---|
| Artificial sweeteners | Sweetness comes from organic agave |
| Proprietary blends | Every functional ingredient and dose is disclosed |
| Artificial colors | Matcha provides natural color |
| Soy or dairy | Allergen-friendly; collagen is the only animal-derived ingredient |

> **Do not claim "no added sugar" or "sugar-free."** Organic agave (10g) is the largest ingredient after water and contributes 8g of added sugars and 35 calories per sachet.
>
> **Do not claim "no preservatives" / "preservative-free."** The formula includes Prolong 2.0, a 2% shelf-life system.

**Allergen note:** Contains no dairy or soy. Made with coconut milk powder — coconut is classified as a tree nut by the FDA, and the product carries a tree-nut cross-contact warning on pack.

## Preparation

1. Tear open one sachet — that's your perfectly measured dose.
2. Pour the liquid concentrate over your milk of choice. Hot or iced.
3. Stir once for a café-grade matcha latte. No powder, no whisk, no mess.

---

## Storage & Shelf Life

- Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight
- Shelf-life system: Prolong 2.0 (see formula)
- Once opened, consume promptly (single-serve format)

## Certifications & Testing (pipeline)

The following are required to substantiate on-pack and site claims — obtain and file before making the associated claim:

- Lion's mane extract CoA (beta-glucan % / assay) — resolves any beta-glucan claim
- Matcha caffeine analysis — substantiates "about 60mg caffeine"
- Matcha origin CoA — substantiates "Kyoto" provenance
- Heavy metals testing (arsenic, lead, cadmium, mercury) per lot
- Microbial testing (finished product) per flavor
- Manufactured in a GMP-certified facility
