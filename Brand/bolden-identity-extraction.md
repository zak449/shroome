## (d) Mé — Character Canon (from guidelines pp. 15–19, 28, 32 + delivered art)

**What Mé is:** a sheep whose face is a mushroom — the sheep references the grass-fed origin of the collagen; the mushroom face references the functional-mushroom ingredients. Rounded, minimal construction matching the logotype. In delivered art: Matcha-green (`#7A881F`) or Green (`#2D341A`) body with Retro-cream mushroom-cap face.

**Rules:**
- Clearspace: the symbol itself is the measurement unit for standalone use. Logotype clearspace uses the lowercase "h" as the unit.
- Approved colorways only, from the official palette; prioritize placement on Green or Purple institutional backgrounds.
- **Prohibited:** stretching/compressing, altering proportions, outlines/outline versions, drop shadows or effects, rotation, combining symbols, off-palette recolors. The mark stays solid and unchanged.
- **Encouraged:** beyond the static symbol, Mé is a character — expressive poses and variations (walking, tumbling, jumping, pouring, holding a cup) used for playful scenes and pattern fills (see eyepatch pattern, box panels, sachet how-to icons). Patterns may be masked *inside* the sheep silhouette.
- Wool texture: a subtle "sheep wool" grain effect may be applied to graphic elements/imagery — keep refined, low intensity. (Effect preset lives in a Bolden Drive folder we do NOT have access to — see gaps.)
- Copy voice around Mé leans on sheep/matcha puns: "Get sheep done", "Sheep happens", "Matcha for mé? the answer is always.", "Our vibe is matcha-in".

**Where Mé appears in delivered work:** sachet front (perched on the sunburst mound), sachet back (3 how-to poses: tear/pour/stir), box front (tumbling with strawberries / with vanilla), box side (jumping with claim stickers), box back (row of mini poses), inside-lid QR moment, keychain (molded rubber form), hat embroidery, eyepatch pattern. Use on web accordingly: playful accents, loaders, empty states, pattern strips — never distorted, never outlined, never rotated.
