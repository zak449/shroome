## 1. Teardowns

### 1.1 Vacation Inc. (vacation.inc) — *from training knowledge, verify before citing externally*

The world-building benchmark. Sunscreen sold as a fictional 1980s leisure corporation.

- **Hero formula:** Full commitment to the bit above the fold — retro resort art direction, product framed as an artifact of the fictional company ("The World's Best-Smelling Sunscreen" positioning). The universe *is* the hero; the SPF facts ride along.
- **CTA:** Simple shop CTAs; the famous capture hook is not "subscribe" but **join the company** — sign up and get a fictional Vacation Inc. job title and business card. Identity as the incentive, not a discount.
- **Capture mechanics:** Email capture wrapped in lore (employee enrollment). The reward is status inside the universe. Massively shareable — people post their fake business cards.
- **Scarcity vs discounts:** Premium held through kitsch and desire, not markdowns. Limited/novelty SKU launches create talkability instead of urgency timers.
- **Lore devices:** Fictional corporate org, era-accurate design (airbrush illustration, retro serif type, film-grain photography), in-world copywriting that never breaks character, absurd brand extensions played straight.
- **Visual discipline:** One era, zero drift. Every touchpoint — type, grain, color, props — passes the "could this exist in 1986?" test.
- **Steal:** The identity-reward capture (join something, get a *thing* with your name on it). Total commitment to one world. Copy that never winks at its own joke.
- **Avoid:** Their nostalgia register. shroomé's world is mé's pastoral-cute lavender field, present-tense Gen-Z — borrowing retro kitsch would smear the identity.

### 1.2 Rhode (rhodeskin.com) — *from training knowledge, verify before citing externally*

The sellout-as-strategy benchmark. Closest structural comp to shroomé's funnel.

- **Hero formula:** Radical minimalism — one product, full-bleed dewy imagery, a few lowercase words. Restraint reads as confidence and premium.
- **CTA:** "shop now" / **"notify me"** — when a hero SKU is sold out, the notify CTA takes the hero slot. Sold-out is presented as the brand's normal state, not an apology.
- **Capture mechanics:** Waitlist/restock notify per product; email + SMS list; restock announcements drive spike days. The list *is* the launch channel.
- **Scarcity vs discounts:** Almost never discounts. Scarcity is real (repeat sellouts of hero SKUs) and reported as fact. Limited flavor drops (seasonal editions) create collectible energy on a small SKU count.
- **Lore devices:** Founder-as-proof, a signature accessory (the lip-case) as a cultural object, lowercase voice, "one of everything" bundle as the canonical order.
- **Visual discipline:** Tight neutral palette (putty/gray/cream), one type voice, macro skin-texture photography, product always lit the same way. Flavor editions get a color moment; the system never moves.
- **Steal:** Notify-me as the hero CTA while sold out. Restock-as-event cadence. Discount abstinence. One canonical bundle. Palette discipline (their putty = our lavender).
- **Avoid:** Founder-dependence (shroomé's face is mé, not a person). Their minimalism is near-silent — shroomé's voice is louder, stickered, playful; don't confuse discipline with mute.

### 1.3 Magna (drinkmagna.com) — *from training knowledge, LOW confidence — verify before using at all*

Fetch blocked and training coverage is thin; do not repeat anything here externally. Directionally: a Gen-Z, creator/community-led functional beverage playing the modern drop/community playbook (SMS-list culture, limited runs, native-to-TikTok launch energy) rather than the retail-shelf playbook. **Action:** someone with an open browser runs the manual teardown checklist below and replaces this section.

Manual teardown checklist (10 min, use for any new comp):
1. Screenshot above the fold, mobile + desktop.
2. Record exact primary CTA text and every competing CTA visible.
3. Trigger the capture flow — email or phone first? incentive offered? how many steps?
4. Sold out or in stock? Any timers/counters — and do they appear real?
5. Any discount surface (welcome %, bundle, subscribe & save)?
6. Name the lore devices: mascot, fictional world, founder, community name, drop numbering.
7. Count palette colors and type styles above the fold.
8. Note product-photo treatment: angle, light, background, props.

### 1.4 Graza (graza.co) — *from training knowledge, verify before citing externally*

The single-object, loud-voice benchmark.

- **Hero formula:** The squeeze bottle — one instantly ownable object — front and center, with names-by-use SKUs ("Sizzle" for cooking, "Drizzle" for finishing). The product's format *is* the pitch, exactly like shroomé's pour-over-milk sachet.
- **CTA:** Direct commerce ("Shop", get-both bundle). Low ceremony, fast to cart.
- **Capture mechanics:** Email list carried by a genuinely entertaining editorial voice — people read it for fun, capture is a byproduct.
- **Scarcity vs discounts:** Mostly neither — the bundle ("get both") and refill pouches do the AOV work instead of promos.
- **Lore devices:** Hand-drawn doodle illustration, irreverent plain-talk copy, anti-pretension stance in a category drowning in pretension.
- **Visual discipline:** One chartreuse owns the brand; doodles + bold type never break style; photography is messy-kitchen-real, not marble-slab aspirational.
- **Steal:** Format-first hero (the pour shot as our squeeze bottle). Two-SKU clarity with personality per flavor. The "get both" canonical bundle. Doodle/sticker energy that stays on-system — mé plays this role.
- **Avoid:** Their always-in-stock grocery rhythm. shroomé is a drop brand; copy the voice, not the availability model.

### 1.5 Poppi (drinkpoppi.com) — *from training knowledge, verify before citing externally*

The flavor-color-system benchmark — and the cautionary tale.

- **Hero formula:** Wall-of-color can lineup, big benefit claim, mass-scale social proof. Built for ubiquity, not scarcity.
- **CTA:** Shop/subscribe paths, variety packs pushed hard.
- **Capture mechanics:** Standard welcome-offer email/SMS popups (discount-incentivized).
- **Scarcity vs discounts:** Discount-led — subscribe & save, bundle deals, promo cycles. The opposite pole from Rhode.
- **Lore devices:** Flavor-as-identity ("which flavor are you"), creator/UGC volume, mainstream celebrity moments.
- **Visual discipline:** The one thing to study — **each flavor owns exactly one color, applied with total consistency** across can, page, ad, shelf. You can name the flavor from a blurred thumbnail.
- **Steal:** Flavor-color law enforced everywhere (maps to our rule: pink = strawberry only, and nothing else is ever pink). Flavor-as-personality copywriting.
- **Avoid:** Everything discount-shaped: welcome-% popups, subscribe & save framing, promo-calendar energy. One welcome-discount popup would delete shroomé's access economy.

## 2. Synthesis — The 10 Rules of the shroomé Funnel

1. **One CTA per view.** Every screenful has exactly one action, and between drops it is always the same action: get on the list. Nav, hero, exit popup, and footer may not ask for different things.
2. **One verb phrase owns the funnel.** Pick the canonical CTA string ("Get first access →") and use it verbatim everywhere between drops. "Restock alerts," "notify me," and "join the waitlist" are ghosts of weaker brands — kill the drift.
3. **Access, never discount.** shroomé never shows a %-off, welcome offer, or promo code on site. The only currencies are earlier access, guaranteed allocation, and referral credit denominated in dollars usable on drop day. (Rhode model; Poppi is the anti-pattern.)
4. **Velocity receipts, not adjectives.** Proof is always a number with a date attached: "Drop 001 — 500 boxes — gone in 9 days." Never "selling fast," never "almost gone" without inventory truth behind it. Every claim must survive a screenshot.
5. **Never fake urgency. Ever.** No countdown that isn't tied to a real event, no fabricated stock levels, no evergreen "ends tonight." A drop brand's only asset is that its scarcity is believed. One fake timer bankrupts it. (Honesty rule — non-negotiable.)
6. **SMS is the front row.** Texts fire before email on every drop, and the site says so at capture ("Drop texts go out before the email"). Phone capture is pitched as an upgrade in access, not a marketing opt-in. Long-term: phone-first capture.
7. **mé is a character, not clip-art.** The sheep appears where a character acts: guiding the ritual steps, celebrating a signup, stamping a drop. Never as filler on an empty corner. mé keeps fixed proportions, faces the content, and speaks only in the brand voice.
8. **The list is a place you're *in*, not a form you filled.** Members are "the flock," they hold a numbered spot, they can move up by referring, and they hear it from us first. Post-signup states must feel like membership (position, perks, share link) — never a "thanks for subscribing" dead end. (Vacation's business-card trick, translated.)
9. **Color law is absolute.** Lavender #E3D5F7 leads, forest #2D341A is the only ink, matcha #7A881F is the only action color (CTAs and nothing else), cream #FEFFF8 is the canvas. Pink appears only when strawberry is on stage. A fifth color on a page is a bug.
10. **UGC is staged in tiers.** Drop 001 buyer photos → curated wall ("The flock has spoken") with names + drop numbers → creator seeding at Drop 002 → customer pour-videos as the primary ad unit by Drop 003. Real people, real pours, real drop numbers — no stock, no paid-review energy.

## 4. Drop 002 Experiment Backlog (prioritized)

1. **P1 — Live list counter (real data).** Replace static "Join 100+ already in line" with a live count from the waitlist API, displayed only once it clears a credibility floor (~250); below the floor, show the Drop 001 velocity receipt instead. Never seeded, never inflated — it's rule 4 made visible. Instrument: signup conversion vs current static line.
2. **P2 — SMS-early mechanics, made explicit.** Test "Text goes out 1 hour before email" as a concrete promise at the phone step (vs current vague "before the email"), plus a phone-first capture variant on the /lp paid-traffic pages. Metric: phone-capture rate, drop-day conversion by channel.
3. **P3 — Numbered spot + queue position.** On signup, show "You're #312 in line for Drop 002" and let referrals move you up (existing referral system already counts — surface it as position). Turns the list into a place (rule 8). Metric: referral share rate.
4. **P4 — UGC wall from Drop 001.** Replace the three text testimonials with a photo/video wall of real Drop 001 customers (name, city, drop number, pour shot), sourced via post-purchase email + credit incentive. Also fixes the thin-proof problem (3 quotes, 12-review schema). Metric: scroll-depth-to-signup on the proof section.
5. **P5 — Drop-countdown, honesty-compliant.** Once the Drop 002 date is truly fixed and inventory allocated, run a real countdown on /drop and the announcement bar — tied to the actual go-live timestamp, disappearing at zero into the live sale. No date, no timer. Pre-registered kill rule: if the date slips, the timer is corrected publicly, not quietly reset. Metric: list-to-purchase conversion on drop day.

Deferred: flavor-match quiz funnel ("Which pour are you?") — good lore vehicle, but with 2 SKUs it's a toy; revisit at 3+ flavors.

## Appendix — Current-site audit notes (localhost:3107 / app/page.tsx, 2026-07-23)

Strong: single conversion goal, velocity receipts in three places, "Sold out. Not gone." framing, referral system with dollar-credit tiers, flavor-color law respected (blush only on strawberry card), mé used behaviorally in the ritual section.

Gaps feeding the backlog:
- **Proof is asserted, not receipted.** "Join 100+ already in line" is a weak static claim; 3 anonymousish testimonials back a 5.0/12-review schema rating. → P1, P4.
- **CTA string drift.** "Get restock alerts →" (nav, twice) vs "Get first access →" (form) vs "Text me first →" — three phrases for one action; nav also leaks attention to 6 destinations pre-capture. → Rule 2, checklist #2.
- **SMS is a skippable afterthought.** Phone ask sits behind email + captcha interrupt with a visible "Skip." For a drop brand the phone list is the asset. → P2.
