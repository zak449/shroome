# shroomé Collab Pipeline

> Last updated: July 2026
> Owner: Business Development
> Purpose: Partnership inspiration bank, prioritized by **effort-to-heat ratio**. Every entry was verified as a real, active brand/venue with recent (2025–2026) collab behavior where findable. No contact names invented — outreach channels are the partner's own published ones.
> Effort key: **S** = existing assets + art handoff only · **M** = one new production run or venue ops · **L** = licensing, co-manufacturing, or retail coordination

---

## How to read this

shroomé's collab thesis: **we are a drop brand that happens to be a beverage.** Anything we co-release should behave like a colorway, not a promotion — numbered, scarce, Mé-fronted, sunburst-framed. The best partners already run their own drop calendars, so a shroomé collab slots into machinery they've built (Olipop does candles, Starface does Snoopy, Igloo runs a literal "COOLaborations" program). We are the small-batch matcha character brand they'd say yes to on vibe alone.

⭐ = one of the **5 easiest wins** (low production lift, high vibe match, plausible yes).

## Guardrails — what a collab can never break

1. **Mé is never distorted or off-palette.** No partner restyling of the character: no new facial features, no squash/stretch, no recolors outside the approved palette (Navy #1B1F3B, Cream #FDF4EE, Lime #C8FF3A, Pink #FFB7D1, Lavender #D4B8E0). Partner-colorway keychains recolor the *body*, never the face. Mé never endorses the partner's functional claims — Mé holds products, Mé doesn't promise outcomes.
2. **No powder imagery, ever.** shroomé is liquid rip-&-pour; any co-created content showing scoops, powder clouds, whisks-with-powder, or "mixing" visuals is rejected at storyboard stage. Partner matcha content must show pour, not powder.
3. **Claims regime is non-negotiable** (see `Product/Compliance & Claims/claims-guidelines.md`). Co-branded copy uses only approved structure/function claims ("supports sustained focus," "supports skin health") with the FDA disclaimer where required. No "boosts immunity," no "clinically proven," no disease or drug-alternative language — even in the partner's caption, even in their voice. shroomé BD reviews every caption before it posts.
4. **Sachet artwork is locked (R02).** The sachet face is never redesigned, over-printed, or co-logo'd. Co-branding lives on outer sleeves, bands, cartons, inserts, and cards — never on the sachet itself. One SKU face, everywhere, always.
5. **Drops stay drops.** No collab unit goes to evergreen/always-in-stock, no un-numbered overruns, no partner-side restock after sell-out. Scarcity is the brand promise; a "restocked collab" breaks every prior buyer's trust.
