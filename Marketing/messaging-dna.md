## 1. The girl (our customer portrait)

**Who she is.** 21 to 28, feminine, extremely online, taste-driven. She has a matcha order, not
just a coffee order. She's the friend who found the thing before it was everywhere: Rhode when it
was one lip case, Vacation before the ads. She spends real money on few, considered things, and
the packaging matters as much as the product. Lavender is her color this year and she knows it.

**What she screenshots.** Sold-out pages of brands she loves ("told you"). A perfect pour shot,
jade over milk. Restock texts, to forward to the group chat. Member-only anything with her name
on it. Cute mascots doing one specific funny thing. She does not screenshot 20%-off popups.

**Why she joined.** She missed the first run, or barely caught it, and she wants the next one.
Signing up is her saying "I'm serious." She's not subscribing to a newsletter; she's holding a
place. She joined a small brand on purpose, so she expects to be talked to like a person by a
person.

**What makes her feel prioritized.** Hearing it first, for real (early window before go-live).
Real numbers and real status, told plainly. A vote that actually decides something. Merch she
can't buy, only earn. Silence between news. A founder who says thank you and means it.

**What makes her feel spammed.** Daily sends. Fake countdowns. Riddles that waste her tap.
"Last chance!!" for the third time. Lore she has to decode to find the information. Discounts
that tell her the brand doesn't believe in itself.

## 2. Messaging hierarchy (one page)

### Core promise
**You come first. We made something worth waiting for, and you'll get it before anyone else,
and we will never waste your inbox to say so.**

### Three proof pillars
1. **The sellout is real.** The first run was 500 boxes and poured out in 9 days. Real numbers
   with dates, always. Never "selling fast," never a fake timer. (Proof of demand.)
2. **The product is real.** 2.5g ceremonial matcha, 200mg fruiting-body extracts at 70%+
   beta-glucans, 2g collagen, already liquid. No powder, no whisk. The stir is the recipe.
   (Proof of substance.)
3. **The membership is real.** Early access a full day before go-live, a vote that decides the
   next flavor, member-only limited-edition merch that is never sold, only earned. (Proof of
   priority.)

### Voice rules
- **Personal.** Emails are short notes from zak, a founder who means it. "hey, it's zak" is a
  legitimate opener. Sign off like a person.
- **Direct.** Say the thing in the first two lines. Information first, charm second.
- **Warm and passionate.** "thank you" and "we worked hard on this" are on-brand sentences.
  Gratitude is a feature, not filler.
- **Cute and funny, in doses.** At most ONE playful wink per message. mé the sheep may appear
  as a cute beat (she taste-tests, she counts votes, she's thrilled). mé is a character we love,
  not a mythology the reader must learn.
- **Short sentences.** Lowercase-cool in email/SMS body copy. Read it out loud; if you wouldn't
  text it, cut it.
- **Never:** lore-speak (ledgers, dispatches, seals, archives), dispatch numbering, riddles,
  em-dashes, fake urgency, fake counts, fake dates, fake member numbers, discount-led framing.
- **Honesty rule stays absolute.** Real numbers only, from `app/lib/drop-config.ts`. If we don't
  know, we say "we don't know yet" and that we'd rather say nothing than guess.
- Sold out = "poured out". "the first run / the next run" in customer copy (never Drop 001/002).
  Tagline "Pour. Swirl. Go." appears once, exactly, never modified.

### The consistency system: 5 phrases, used verbatim everywhere
Email, SMS, site, packaging inserts, social captions. Same words every time, so at 5,000 or
5,000,000 customers the brand still sounds like one person.

1. **"you hear it first."** (the access promise; every early-access mention)
2. **"we only email when it matters. no spam, ever."** (the anti-spam promise; email footer,
   welcome, capture forms)
3. **"the first run poured out in 9 days."** (the velocity receipt; long form: "500 boxes,
   poured out in 9 days")
4. **"the stir is the recipe."** (the product in five words)
5. **"never sold, only earned."** (member merch, always)
