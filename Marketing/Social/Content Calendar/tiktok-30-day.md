## posting cadence & timing

- **1 post/day, 30 days straight.** tiktok first; repurpose to reels 24–48h later.
- **timing:** pour/ASMR/GRWM content posts **7–8am ET** (catch the morning-scroll audience mid-craving). education, rants, and founder content post **6–8pm ET** (longer watch-time window). waitlist-crescendo posts in week 4 post **7am ET sharp** — train the audience that shroomé news arrives with their morning.
- **batching:** film all ASMR pour days in one shoot (same setup, same glass, same light — repetition builds the recognizable asset). film both founder days in one sitting for consistent energy.
- **weekly spine:** wk1 world-building + sold-out story → wk2 ingredient science made delicious → wk3 pour-identity + UGC bait → wk4 waitlist FOMO crescendo + restock teasers.

## 5 evergreen hook formulas (reusable forever)

1. **the forbidden object** — show the thing they can't buy. "this is the drink you can't get right now." scarcity does the work; you just hold the sachet.
2. **the correction** — open by fixing a wrong assumption. "it's not a powder. it was never a powder." people stay to argue or to learn.
3. **the hyper-specific number** — "2.5g. 2g. 200mg. 60mg. one sachet." specificity reads as receipts; vagueness reads as marketing.
4. **the insider leak** — "this is everything i'm allowed to tell you about the restock." implies a rope line; the viewer is suddenly inside it.
5. **the sensory dare** — no voice, one amplified sound (the tear, the pour, the swirl), one word of text: "listen." the anti-hook hook; it stops the scroll by dropping the volume of the feed.

## comment-management playbook (first hour = the algorithm hour)

- **0–15 min:** founder or social lead replies to the first 10–20 comments — real sentences, lowercase, warm. no copy-paste. the algorithm reads reply velocity as signal.
- **pin strategy:** pin the funniest or most-relatable comment, not the brand's own. cult brands let the community hold the mic.
- **seed the cta:** brand account leaves one comment on every post — "waitlist is open at drinkshroome.com. the next pour drops soon." — and pins it only on week-4 posts.
- **video replies:** harvest questions all month; answer the two best as video-reply posts (days 10 and 17 are built for this — keep slots flexible to swap in whatever question is actually blowing up).
- **compliance in comments:** never answer health-outcome questions with promises. if someone asks "will this fix my anxiety/skin/adhd" reply: "we can't make medical claims — but here's what's inside and what the ingredients are studied for 🌿" and point to the ingredient posts. never say cure/treat/prevent/heal, even casually.
- **"is this a scam / fake sold out" comments:** answer transparently and fast — "real sold out, unfortunately. small first run, small team. receipts on the way in friday's video." defensiveness kills; documentation converts.
- **negative taste/price comments:** don't delete. reply with honesty ("it's $x because ceremonial + fruiting-body extract are genuinely expensive — friday's video shows why"). screenshot-worthy replies are content.
