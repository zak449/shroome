## 0. Standing rules (apply to every day)

- **Compliance:** structure/function claims only ("supports immune function," "supports sustained focus," "supports healthy energy levels," "supports skin health," "supports gut health," "provides antioxidant support"). Any post whose caption or on-screen text makes one of these claims must carry the FDA disclaimer in the caption:
  > *these statements have not been evaluated by the food and drug administration. this product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.*
  Entries below are flagged **[FDA disclaimer]** where required. Never: "boosts immunity," "cures," "treats," "clinically proven," drug comparisons.
- **No urgency/scarcity language** (per content guardrails). The restock countdown is framed as *information and celebration* — "here's the date, waitlist walks in first" — never "hurry / almost gone / don't miss out."
- **Visuals:** real sachets only (locked R02 designs: vanilla = Retro off-white #FEFFF8 w/ Green #2D341A serif wordmark + Vanilla #E4CB9F flavor tag; strawberry = Retro off-white w/ Green serif wordmark + Strawberry #FF6DC7 flavor tag). The product is a **1 fl oz dark-green liquid concentrate** — never show powder, whisks, scoops, or ceremony imagery. Any cup on camera says "shroomé." Product shots use starburst lighting; lifestyle shots are warm, candid, lived-in. Cloud backgrounds (purple/strawberry-tint) for graphic tiles.
- **Type on tiles:** Instrument Serif italic for display lines, Syne bold for labels/CTAs. CTA chips invert to Green #2D341A surface with Retro #FEFFF8 text; Matcha #7A881F only as accent fills on Green — never Matcha text on Retro, never Strawberry + Matcha adjacency.
- **Every day gets stories** (2–4 frames), even feed-rest days. Every story set ends with a link sticker to the waitlist unless noted.
- **Posting times:** feed 8–9am ET; stories staggered 8am / 12pm / 6pm.
