## 1. the magna funnel thesis

Context that matters: Magna is **not a matcha brand** — it's Michael Preysman's (Everlane founder) magnesium-electrolyte hydration brand, launched July 2024, branding by agency Some Days, explicitly aimed at gen-Z wellness (✅ verified). The founder's instinct is right anyway: Preysman ran Everlane's "radical transparency" DTC playbook for a decade, and Magna is that playbook rebuilt for functional beverages. What transfers is the **offer architecture**, not the category.

### 1a. What makes it work

**One-ingredient story, owned completely.** "The world's first magnesium-powered hydration formula" (✅). Preysman picked magnesium because it's the most-searched nutrient/mineral on Google (✅ per Fast Company/New Consumer) — the positioning IS the SEO/demand-capture strategy. Three magnesium forms, each mapped to a benefit: L-threonate → focus, malate → energy, glycinate → recovery (✅). One hero claim, three sub-claims, zero blend soup. They also maintain a dedicated `/pages/magnesium` education page (✅ URL verified in search index) — the ad claim has a canonical place to be explained.

**Subscription-first price architecture.** 30 servings: **$55 one-time vs $45 with subscription** (✅ — an $10/~18% delta). The one-time price exists mainly to make the subscription look correct. Product framed as "a habitual part of someone's daily routine" (✅ New Consumer) — habit framing justifies auto-ship.

**Starter Kit as the conversion offer.** A **$39 one-box Starter Kit (28 servings, $1.40/serving)** that includes a **free water bottle + 2 complimentary flavor packs when taken with a subscription**; a $70 two-box version at $1.25/serving (✅ per Let's Live It Up 2026 review). Anatomy of the move: the free physical gift (bottle) is (a) a subscription bribe, (b) a habit anchor — the branded bottle on your desk is a daily reorder ad, and (c) perceived-value padding that avoids discounting the consumable itself. Flavor packs de-risk the flavor-commitment objection.

**Ad → dedicated LP congruence.** Search surfaced standalone funnel pages: `/pages/the-magna-bundle`, `/pages/welcome-to-magna`, `/pages/referral/free-8-count-sampler-pack` (✅ URLs verified). This is the classic paid-social structure: each offer/audience gets its own page rather than dumping traffic on the PDP. (Page contents ⚠ inferred — blocked from fetching.)

**Referral paid in product, not percent.** The referral reward page is literally named "free-8-count-sampler-pack" (✅ URL). Product-as-currency: a sampler costs them COGS, feels like a gift, and creates a *second* trial funnel out of every advocate.

**Perpetual light discounting.** Coupon aggregators show 3–4 verified codes live at any time averaging ~15% off (✅ SimplyCodes). A permanent "there's always a code" layer that harvests deal-intent traffic without touching the headline price.

**What we could NOT verify live** (⚠ inferred from DTC pattern + reviewer descriptions): sticky add-to-cart, review count/density on PDP, UGC volume, comparison-vs-Gatorade/LMNT table, quiz. Reviewers describe heavy paid-social targeting ("Magna had been targeting them with their ads" ✅) and largely positive reviews with flavor-split criticism (✅). Assume a competent Shopify PDP; do not assume specifics.

### 1b. The transferable thesis in one line
**Pick the one ingredient people already search, own its education, price the subscription as the default, convert with a kitted starter offer + physical gift, and give every ad and referral its own dedicated page.**

## 2. the seed funnel thesis

Seed (DS-01 Daily Synbiotic) is the science-authority playbook: they sell *precision and credibility* and let the subscription be the only way to buy.

### 2a. What makes it work

**Education-first everything.** The brand thesis is that the probiotic category is junk science, and Seed is the corrective. PDP and content lead with mechanism (strains, survivability, AFU dosing) rather than benefits (⚠ inferred structure; consistent with all cited analyses). Their content moat extends to consumer-facing curriculum and email that teaches before it sells (✅ Incrementum: retention emails are educational, e.g. the "66 days to form a habit" email at month two).

**Quiz-gated onboarding.** "Is DS-01 Right For You?" quiz (✅ live page at seed.com/is-ds01-right-for-you) — questions on current supplements, digestive symptoms, health goals (✅ HealthRX). Function: (1) personalization data, (2) commitment escalation before the price reveal, (3) an email-capture device that arrives pre-segmented.

**Subscription-only — no decision fatigue.** You cannot one-time-purchase DS-01: **15% off the first shipment, then $49.99/month** (✅ DTC Patterns / HealthRX). No cadence matrix, no tier math — one product, one price, one rhythm. The refill model does the retention work.

**The welcome-kit / refill ritual.** First shipment: branded reusable glass jar + glass travel vial in mushroom/cornstarch packaging; every subsequent shipment is a compostable biofilm refill (✅ Incrementum). This is the single smartest retention mechanic in DTC: the durable jar makes canceling feel like abandoning an object you own; the refill makes each renewal cheaper to ship, sustainable (brand story), and visually incomplete without the jar. Sustainability isn't CSR — it's the retention device.

**Affiliate rigor as brand armor.** Influencers must complete **SeedUniversity** — a 59-minute course on microbiome science, probiotic mechanisms, and FTC compliance — and **pass an exam before they may promote** (✅ Incrementum). Result: zero rogue claims, and the gate itself is PR ("the brand that makes influencers pass a science test"). They've since exported the model to retail: the Erewhon partnership pays the retailer as a lead-gen affiliate when in-store customers convert to subscriptions (✅ Modern Retail).

**Retention loop mechanics** (✅/⚠ mixed): habit-milestone emails (66-day, ✅), education cadence in email (✅), community/digest content and a general "PhD tone" (⚠ inferred). Widely reported 30-day risk-free guarantee (⚠ inferred — not verified this pass; confirm before citing).

### 2b. The transferable thesis in one line
**Make the label a spec sheet, gate the funnel with a quiz, sell only the ritual (refill subscription + durable first-order object), and force everyone who talks about you to pass your science test.**

## 4. prioritized upgrade plan (max 10)

Legend: effort S/M/L · **NOW** = buildable on the Next.js waitlist site today · **SHOPIFY** = needs the store live.

### ★ BUILD TODAY #1 — "find your pour" quiz → Klaviyo properties
- **What:** 4-question quiz (flavor / temp / ritual time / current drink) as `/quiz`, posting to the existing waitlist API + Klaviyo (`flavor_pref`, `temp_pref`, `ritual_time`, `current_drink`, `pour_profile`), rewarding the specced "+50 spots" and a shareable "first pour profile" card. Wire as the post-signup step on all 5 LPs and Email 3's destination.
- **Steals:** Seed's quiz-gated onboarding ("Is DS-01 Right For You?").
- **Impact:** turns every signup from an email into a segmented profile; unlocks the already-written FLOW C and flavor-segmented drop-day creative; quiz completers are the pre-committed cohort for drop 002.
- **Effort:** S–M · **NOW** (everything downstream is already specced in engagement-capture-flows.md; this is the missing artifact).

### ★ BUILD TODAY #2 — /drop → PDP-grade sold-out page
- **What:** upgrade /drop from poster to salesperson: (a) ingredient **evidence accordion** per active (matcha grade, collagen peptide science, ≥70% beta-glucan fruiting-body explainer with links to the existing SEO blog posts + a COA/third-party-testing block), (b) **comparison table** — "shroomé vs matcha powder vs café order vs mushroom coffee" on prep time, matcha dose, disclosed doses, $/serving, (c) sticky bottom CTA bar ("drop 002 — get access") on scroll, (d) drop-001 sell-through fact as proof module.
- **Steals:** Magna's education-backed PDP + Seed's label-as-spec-sheet; sticky ATC pattern.
- **Impact:** the page every ad, email, and referral ultimately lands on starts converting skeptics, not just believers; comparison table is also the ad-creative source of truth.
- **Effort:** M · **NOW** (content exists in segment docs + SEO posts; pure frontend).

### ★ BUILD TODAY #3 — post-signup share takeover ("you're #214 in line")
- **What:** replace the small referral box with a full-screen post-signup moment: waitlist position + pour-profile card + referral link + "each friend = closer to drop 002 + $5/$10/$15" with milestone progress bar; same module as the quiz result screen. Add position-jump for shares (consistent with the +50-spots economy).
- **Steals:** Magna's product-as-currency referral energy (and every waitlist brand's Harry's moment) applied to our capped-credit ladder.
- **Impact:** the only compounding growth loop available while sold out; raises K-factor on existing traffic at zero media cost.
- **Effort:** S · **NOW** (referral API, tiers, and share handlers already exist — this is UI + one position-jump rule).

### 4. Drop 002 "first pour kit" offer with a physical anchor
- Named starter kit (e.g. trial 6 + glass or cup + sticker sheet) where the gift is **subscription-conditional**, à la Magna's water bottle; the durable object doubles as Seed's jar logic. **Effort M · SHOPIFY** (spec + creative NOW).

### 5. Subscription = guaranteed allocation, everywhere
- Promote the sub matrix's real hero — "subscribers never miss a drop" — into every sold-out card, the waitlist confirmation, and Email 5; collect "notify me about subscriptions" intent property now. Steals Seed's no-decision-fatigue default + Magna's sub-first delta. **Effort S · NOW (framing) / SHOPIFY (checkout).**

### 6. Evidence & testing page (/lab or /receipts)
- One page: COAs, beta-glucan third-party results, heavy-metals testing, GMP facility, dosage rationale with citations. Every "doses on the label" claim links here. Steals Seed's authority moat; the focus segment converts on this alone. **Effort M · NOW** (needs real docs from ops — start with what exists, never fabricate).

### 7. Education nurture arc (Emails 7–10)
- 4-email ingredient curriculum (why ceremonial grade / fruiting-body vs mycelium / caffeine+L-theanine curve / collagen honesty incl. the "2g is a maintenance dose" straight-talk) slotted between Flow A day 4 and day 21; repurposes 3 existing SEO posts. Steals Seed's teach-before-sell retention email DNA. **Effort S–M · NOW** (Klaviyo copy work).

### 8. Creator program with a "pour test" gate
- Micro-version of SeedUniversity: a 10-minute brief + quiz (claims rules from segment-strategy.md §compliance) every creator/affiliate must pass; certificate = flex content. Protects us in the highest-risk segment (calm/anxiety language). **Effort M · NOW to spec, ongoing to run.**

### 9. Post-purchase habit flow for drop 002
- Day 0 unboxing ritual card, day 7 check-in, day 30 "your first 12 pours" recap, day 66 habit-milestone email (Seed verbatim steal), timed against sachets-remaining math to land reorder/subscribe nudge before they run dry. **Effort M · SHOPIFY** (write NOW, arm at launch).

### 10. Refill / packaging story for drop 003+
- Longer-term: durable canister + lighter refill packs; sustainability narrative doing retention work. Seed's deepest moat, our furthest-out steal. **Effort L · SHOPIFY+ops.**

## 5. bottom line

Magna's funnel wins on **offer architecture** (starter kit + gift + sub-default pricing + dedicated LPs); Seed's wins on **authority and retention physics** (quiz, spec-sheet PDP, refill ritual, gated affiliates). Shroomé already matches both on ad→LP discipline and honest-scarcity mechanics — the funnel breaks in the middle (a /drop page that displays instead of persuades, a signup that captures no segmentation) and after the signup (a share moment that whispers). Items 1–3 fix exactly that, this week, without Shopify.
